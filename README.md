# Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics 

An end-to-end sales analytics project focused on transforming cleaned transactional data into actionable business insights using **Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**.

The project analyzes revenue trends, product performance, payment behavior, and delivery outcomes to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tech Stack

* **Python**
* **Pandas** – Data manipulation, aggregation, and time-series analysis
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
* **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis and visualization
* **CSV** – Source dataset

## Key Analysis

The project explores the following business questions:

* How does **revenue change month over month**?
* Which **product categories generate the most revenue**?
* Which **payment methods contribute the highest revenue**?
* What is the distribution of **delivery statuses**?
* Which **products have the highest sales volume**?

## Visualizations

The analysis includes:

1. **Monthly Revenue Trend**
   Tracks revenue over time to identify growth patterns, fluctuations, and seasonal trends.

2. **Top 5 Product Categories by Revenue**
   Identifies the highest-revenue categories and highlights key contributors to overall sales.

3. **Revenue by Payment Method**
   Compares revenue generated through different payment channels.

4. **Delivery Status Distribution**
   Examines order fulfillment outcomes such as delivered, pending, or cancelled orders.

5. **Top 10 Best-Selling Products**
   Ranks products based on total quantity sold to identify high-demand products.

## Business Value

The analysis can help stakeholders:

* Monitor **revenue performance and trends**
* Identify **high-performing product categories**
* Understand **customer purchasing patterns**
* Evaluate **payment channel performance**
* Identify **operational issues through delivery outcomes**
* Prioritize **high-demand products and categories**
