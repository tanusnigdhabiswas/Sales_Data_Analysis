# Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics

An exploratory sales analytics project using **Python and Pandas** to uncover revenue trends, product performance, payment patterns, and delivery outcomes from transactional sales data.

### Tech Stack

**Python | Pandas | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Jupyter Notebook**

### Analysis

* Analyzed **monthly revenue trends** to identify changes in sales performance over time.
* Identified **top-performing product categories and products** based on revenue and quantity sold.
* Compared **revenue across payment methods** to understand channel contribution.
* Examined **delivery status distribution** to identify fulfillment patterns.

### Key Skills

**Data Cleaning | Feature Engineering | Exploratory Data Analysis | Aggregation | Time-Series Analysis | Data Visualization | Business Insights**
